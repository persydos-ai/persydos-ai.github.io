⟢ MISSION · WHY WE EXIST

Intelligence is better
when it's closer to home.

We're building Persydos AI to put real intelligence — not a thin client, not a remote API — into homes, small teams, and independent operators.

The cloud has done extraordinary work to make AI possible at scale, and it will keep doing so. Alongside it, we think there's room — and real demand — for intelligence that lives closer to the people using it: on the phone in your hand, the laptop on the desk, the small box in the closet.

Democratizing AI, to us, simply means more places where compute, memory, and keys can comfortably live. Persydos AI complements the cloud rather than replacing it, and reaches for it only when you ask.
